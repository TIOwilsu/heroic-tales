# 10 — D&D content policy

Heroic Tales supports D&D 5th Edition (2014) and D&D Revised 5th Edition (2024). Rules, mechanical terminology, and content references must be kept separate by edition in storage, prompts, APIs, tests, and UI.

Only official mechanical content may be presented as official, and the product must not fabricate, remix, or imply endorsement of mechanics. Original character stories are permitted within official settings when they do not contradict established canon. Original narrative must be labeled as original narrative; uncertain claims must be labeled unverified.

Copyright, trademark, trade dress, and license permissions vary by source and jurisdiction. Do not assume that every published book may be reproduced, indexed, embedded, or used commercially. Before importing any source, record its provenance, license/permission status, allowed fields, attribution requirements, and removal process. Legal review and the exact licensed corpus are `TBD`.
